# BERIAS Back-Office Health Check

A free 5-minute assessment tool for freight brokers, 3PLs, and carriers to identify operational friction points and evaluate process maturity.

## Live Demo
https://sprint.berias.com/assessment/

## What users get
- Instant maturity score
- Category breakdown
- Tailored recommendations
- Downloadable PDF summary

## Who is this for?
Designed for small and mid-sized freight and logistics businesses that want to optimize their back-office operations.

## Methodology
The Back-Office Health Check evaluates operational process maturity across 15 questions focused on process clarity, documentation, ownership, communication, and consistency. It is designed as a fast diagnostic tool to highlight likely friction points, not as a full consulting audit.
