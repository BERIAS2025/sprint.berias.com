Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the 30-Day Clarity Sprint

What exactly do you do during the 30-day sprint?

+

I am The Architect. I diagnose where your operations are broken, design the systems to fix them, and coach your team through the change. I do not run your business for you. I build the infrastructure so your team can run it without chaos. Think of it like hiring an architect for a building: I draw the blueprints, your crew builds the house.

What does my company need to do?

+

Your team is essential to this process. Here is the split:

  • I do: The operational audit, workflow redesign, dossier framework architecture, training sessions, change management coaching, and the 90-day roadmap.
  • Your team does: Participate in audit interviews (30-60 min each), provide the "how we actually do it" details, attend training sessions, and assign process owners who will drive adoption after the sprint.
  • You (the owner) do: A 60-minute Vision Alignment at the start and a Final Review at the end. That is it, unless you want to be more involved.

Why only 30 days? Is this not a longer project?

+

Most process projects fail because they drag on for months and lose momentum. I use a Sprint Methodology to install the 20% of infrastructure that drives 80% of your results. We lay the tracks in 30 days. Your team uses the 90-day roadmap to keep the train moving. For larger companies (35+ employees), we offer a Phase 2 to go deeper.

What is the "Operational Clarity Dossier"?

+

It is your company's Owner's Manual. A digital, searchable library of how your business actually runs. If your best dispatcher left tomorrow, their replacement could open the Dossier and be 80% productive by lunch on Day 1. I build the architecture and framework. Your team fills in the role-specific details with my guidance. The result is a living document that grows with your company.

How does the Success Guarantee work?

+

Simple. We guarantee we will identify at least three specific, fixable inefficiencies in your freight operation, each with a documented time or cost impact. If we do not, you pay nothing for the Sprint.

We already have a TMS. Do we need to change it?

+

Not necessarily. I will audit what you have and what is working. Often, freight businesses have solid tools but poor integration, poor adoption, or workflows that do not match how the tool was designed to be used. I will optimize what you have, eliminate redundancies, and fill the gaps. You will not be starting from scratch. You will be building on your existing foundation.

How do I know if my business is ready for this?

+

If your dispatchers are spending more time answering status calls than booking loads, if your back-office team is drowning in paperwork, if you cannot answer basic questions about your operation without digging through spreadsheets, you are ready. Book a free 15-minute Process Leak Audit and we will determine together if the Sprint is the right fit for your current stage.
